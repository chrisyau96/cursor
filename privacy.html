Momentum Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Momentum is a habit tracker. The app is designed so your habits, journals, and rewards stay on your device unless you choose to back them up.

Data we store on your device

Habits, completion records, journal entries, reward rules, and settings are saved in on-device storage (and, in the store apps, the app sandbox). We do not require an account.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you connect Google Drive, Momentum uploads an encrypted-in-transit copy of your backup JSON to Google Drive’s hidden app data folder, using the Drive appData scope. We do not receive that file on a Momentum server. You can disconnect Drive and delete the backup from Google’s account tools at any time.

Google sign-in uses a client ID you configure. Tokens stay on your device.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They are not sent through a Momentum push server.

Widgets

Home screen widgets read a snapshot of today’s habits, streak, credits, and gift progress stored on the device. Completing or resetting a habit from a widget writes a pending action on the device, then the app applies it.

Advertising

The free app shows ads (a house banner, and Google AdMob banners in the Play app when configured). Ads are hidden after a one-time in-app purchase (HK$38, lifetime, Google Play Billing).

When AdMob is used, Google may collect the Android advertising ID and approximate device information to serve and measure ads. We do not sell personal data. You can reset or opt out of the advertising ID in Android settings.

In-app purchases

Remove-ads is a one-time Google Play purchase. Google processes payment. Play purchase records are used only to unlock ads-removed on this device and to restore the purchase on another phone signed into the same Google account.

What we do not do

  • No analytics trackers in the core app
  • No sale of personal data

Contact

Questions: open an issue on the project repository or email the developer listed on the store listing.
